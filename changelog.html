    Changelog

    Built in the open, shipped continuously.

    TimingAX is actively developed by a single founder. Here's a running log of what's changed — new features, real data, and fixes.

    Release log16 entries
    1. 26Oct 2026
      New

      Real data everywhere — no modelled figures

      Every figure on TimingAX now comes from real closing prices or an official record. The Backtest buys and sells at actual closes and counts only completed windows. Correlations adds the correlation of two assets’ actual monthly returns. This Month in History shows every year’s result, the biggest daily moves and the Fed decisions in that month. A new market events library covers Fed rate changes, US elections and Bitcoin halvings, and the economic calendar uses official Fed and BLS schedules. Assets without real price history now say so instead of showing estimates, and our research articles were checked line by line against the data.

    2. 25Oct 2026
      New

      A new look, plus projections, leaderboard, compare pages and calendar alerts

      A redesigned site with a live market ticker, and new tools: a seasonal projection for the next 30, 60 or 90 days, a leaderboard of every asset for the month, compare pages for any two assets, weekly and turn-of-month patterns, and an add-to-calendar feed for your watchlist.

    3. 14Jun 2026
      New

      Referral program: give a month, get a month

      Pro and Lifetime users now get a personal referral link from their dashboard. When someone signs up with your link and goes Pro, you both get a free month — tracked automatically and applied as account credit.

    4. 14Jun 2026
      New

      Unique share previews for every asset, plus a privacy notice & accessibility statement

      Every one of our 430+ asset pages now generates its own social share image on the fly — no more generic preview when a link gets shared. We've also added a one-time privacy notice covering our (minimal, tracking-free) use of cookies and local storage, and published an honest accessibility statement covering what works well today and where we still have work to do.

    5. 14Jun 2026
      New

      The Academy: 6 new guides to seasonality

      A new Academy section covers the fundamentals — what seasonality is (and isn't), the September Effect, the Santa Claus Rally, detrending explained, the presidential cycle, and how to use seasonal patterns responsibly. Plain-language, honest about limitations, and linked from every asset page.

    6. 14Jun 2026
      New

      430+ new asset pages & a browsable directory

      Every asset in our catalogue now has its own seasonality snapshot page — chart, best/worst months, full 12-month table, and a VERIFIED/MODELLED badge — plus a new Browse Assets directory grouped by market and sector.

    7. 14Jun 2026
      Major

      10th tool: Portfolio Seasonality, plus detrending, presidential-cycle filters & seasonal alerts

      See your whole portfolio's combined seasonal exposure by entering your holdings and weights — a weighted blend of each asset's own pattern, with a holding-by-holding breakdown. Every chart now supports a Detrend toggle (see relative seasonal strength independent of an asset's overall trend — especially useful for crypto and high-growth stocks) and presidential-cycle filtering (election / pre-election / post-election / midterm years). You can also set up seasonal email alerts to get notified before an asset's historically strongest month begins.

    8. 13Jun 2026
      Major

      All 9 tools now run on real price data

      The Backtest, Screener, Watchlist, and Correlations tools now compute from real historical monthly prices — joining the Analyzer, which already did. Every analysis shows a ✓ VERIFIED or MODELLED badge so you always know which you're looking at. Added a full Data & Methodology page explaining exactly how.

    9. 12Jun 2026
      MajorDesign

      Live interactive demo, real ticker, and a new icon set

      The homepage hero is now a working mini-analyzer — pick an asset and watch real seasonal data render and animate in. The price ticker now pulls live quotes. All emoji icons across the site were replaced with a custom-drawn icon set, and scroll-triggered motion was added throughout.

    10. 12Jun 2026
      FixTrust

      Real contact form & newsletter signup

      The contact form and blog newsletter signup now genuinely store submissions — messages reach a real inbox and subscribers are saved. No more simulated "success" messages.

    11. 12Jun 2026
      Billing

      Self-service subscription management

      Pro and Lifetime members can now manage their subscription — update payment details, view invoices, or cancel — directly from the dashboard via a secure Stripe-hosted portal. No emailing support required.

    12. 12Jun 2026
      Access

      Free tier properly enforced

      Free accounts are now limited to 3 analyses per month and indices/ETFs only, with Backtest, Earnings, Screener, Watchlist, and Correlations clearly marked as Pro features. Previously, logged-in free accounts had unrestricted access — this is now fixed across the board.

    13. 11Jun 2026
      Major

      Live payments are go

      Stripe is live — Pro Monthly (£19.99), Pro Annual (£149), and Lifetime (£299) plans are all purchasable. Subscriptions activate instantly via webhook, updating account access in real time.

    14. 11Jun 2026
      OnboardingPolish

      New user walkthrough, favicon & social previews

      First-time dashboard visitors now get a short interactive walkthrough of the 9 tools and plans. Added a proper site icon (visible in browser tabs and bookmarks) and a branded preview image for links shared on social media.

    15. 10Jun 2026
      AI

      AI chat assistant launched

      Every page now has a chat assistant in the bottom-right corner — ask about pricing, how a tool works, or specific seasonal patterns, and get an instant answer.

    16. 9Jun 2026
      Launch

      TimingAX launches

      Initial launch: 9 seasonal analysis tools covering 460+ global assets across US, UK, German, and Asian markets — Seasonal Analyzer, Strategy Backtest, Event Studies, Earnings Analysis, Seasonal Screener, Watchlist, This Month in History, Correlations, and an Economic Calendar.
