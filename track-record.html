    How this is worked out

    • For each month, the signal uses only the 15 years before it — e.g. the signal for March 2025 comes from March 2010–2024 — so nothing it “knew” came from the future.
    • Tailwind: average at least +0.8% and up in at least 60% of those years. Headwind: average −0.5% or lower and up in 45% or fewer. Otherwise neutral.
    • Outcomes are each month’s real return from month-end closes (the current, unfinished month isn’t included). Markets: S&P 500, NASDAQ Composite, Dow Jones, FTSE 100, DAX 40, Nikkei 225, Gold (GLD), Bitcoin.
    • Past results don’t predict future ones; this is a record of how a historical tendency has played out, not advice. Data & methodology.
