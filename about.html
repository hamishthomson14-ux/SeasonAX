TimingAX exists because one pattern was hiding in plain sight for decades, and almost nobody was making it easy to see.

The origin

It started with a
terrible September.

September 2022. The S&P 500 fell 9.3%. Portfolio down. In pain. And the same thing had happened, to varying degrees, the September before, and the one before that. So the question became: is this just bad luck, or is there something structural here?

Three hours of research later, the answer was unambiguous. For the S&P 500, September is the only month of the year to average a net loss over the last 15 years (−1.24%), and the UK and Germany show September weakness too. Not a coincidence. Not a 2022 anomaly. A pattern academic studies have tracked for decades — though not everywhere: Japan’s Nikkei has tended to rise in September, which is exactly why it pays to check each market’s own calendar.

Then the next question: what are the good months? November — +3.11% average for the S&P 500, a 87% win rate over 15 years. The entire "Best Six Months" from November through April. Bitcoin in October, up in 9 of 13 years. January for small caps. The data was everywhere once you knew where to look.

"The edge wasn't secret. It was just fragmented, scattered across academic papers and institutional research that most investors never see."

The existing tools were either priced for hedge funds (£40k+/year Bloomberg terminals) or too simplistic to be useful. There was no professional-grade seasonal analysis tool built for self-directed investors who take their portfolios seriously. So we built one.

For the S&P 500, September has averaged −1.24% and November +3.11% — a gap of more than four percentage points between two months of the same year. Over a 15-year period, an investor who simply avoided September and was overweight November would have materially outperformed one who held constant positions all year — with less risk, not more. That's the entire premise of TimingAX.

Methodology

What's under the hood.

Every figure on TimingAX is computed from real closing prices or taken from an official record, and the exact method is published on our Data & methodology page. We’re transparent about how it works because transparency is non-negotiable for a financial data product.

📈
Real closing prices
Daily closing prices from Yahoo Finance (adjusted closes where Yahoo provides them), up to 20 years per asset. Monthly averages use up to 15 years; observation counts are shown for every figure.
📚
Official event dates
Fed rate changes from the Federal Reserve (federalreserve.gov), CPI and jobs-report dates from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (bls.gov), US election days from federal law, Bitcoin halvings from the blockchain — each linked to its source.
🌐
No estimates
If we don’t have real price history for an asset, we say so and show no figures for it. Nothing on the platform is filled in from a model.
⏱
Real backtests
Strategy backtests buy and sell at actual closing prices on the trading days you choose, compound the results and count only windows that have finished.
⚠
Explicit caveats
Every analysis output includes explicit limitations. Seasonal patterns are statistical tendencies, not guarantees. We say this loudly, not in tiny print.
🔄
Regular updates
Figures recompute from the latest prices (cached for up to an hour). Event dates are checked against their official schedules.
How we work

The principles we
won't compromise on.

01
No fake testimonials. Ever.
We're a new product. We don't have 500 verified reviews yet. We will not invent them. Our data is the proof of concept — not fabricated quotes from made-up users. When we have real users who choose to share their experience, we'll show them.
02
Explicit limitations on every output.
Seasonal patterns are tendencies, not predictions. Every analysis, backtest and event study on the platform carries an explicit caveat saying exactly that. We designed the product to be useful, not to make you feel more certain than the data justifies.
03
Real data or nothing.
Every number comes from real prices or an official record. Where we don’t have real data for an asset, we say so instead of estimating — no simulated backtests, no modelled patterns, no invented figures.
04
Simple, honest pricing.
Free tier (5 analyses/month, no card needed), Pro at £19.99/month or £149/year with a 7-day free trial. One-click cancellation, no retention friction. We will never introduce dark patterns, manipulative upgrade prompts or misleading annual-versus-monthly pricing sleight of hand.
